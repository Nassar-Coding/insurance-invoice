# Mapping instruction, revision 2

Apply after the first H1 development evaluation exposed an open-catalog error.
Do not assume every billed description is a contracted service: the task includes
unknown services. A single candidate within the supplied catalog is insufficient
when an essential setting, specialty, or service noun is missing.

Retain the candidate search and frozen lookup architecture. Require independent
tokens for essential service-name concepts; a token with two meanings cannot
prove both at once. Permit omission of a trailing generic noun only when the
remaining service phrase retains its defining head (for example recovery room
for recovery room occupancy, or laboratory for laboratory panel). Do not infer
missing specialties, settings, wound from care, or nutritional from support.
Never use a matching billed amount to choose a service; a wrong billed amount
or unit is not proof of another identity. Preserve all unresolved alternatives.

Save the original candidates, previous accepted records, source evidence, revised
mapping records and change decision. Re-evaluate H1 development data and later
the frozen check. Apply the same semantic standard to every target hospital.

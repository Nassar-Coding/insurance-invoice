L795: 34.9 The Parties acknowledge that a provision which is not not waived by any delay or omission in enforcing it may nonetheless be given effect by agreement in writing, but that nothing in this Article obliges either Party to do so.
L797: 34.10 For the avoidance of doubt, nothing in this Article requires a provision to be not waived by any delay or omission in enforcing it where the requirement would be inconsistent with any mandatory legal or regulatory obligation binding on the Party concerned; in that event the Party shall notify the other and the Parties shall agree a compliant alternative.
L799: 34.11 Each provision under this Agreement shall be to be construed so as to give effect to the commercial intention of the Parties.
L801: 34.12 No provision shall be treated as effective for the purposes of this Agreement unless it is to be construed so as to give effect to the commercial intention of the Parties, and the Party relying on it bears the burden of demonstrating that it was.
L803: 34.13 Where a provision is to be construed so as to give effect to the commercial intention of the Parties, the Parties shall each act reasonably and without undue delay in giving effect to it, and neither Party shall unreasonably withhold any consent required in connection with it.
L805: 34.14 The Parties acknowledge that a provision which is not to be construed so as to give effect to the commercial intention of the Parties may nonetheless be given effect by agreement in writing, but that nothing in this Article obliges either Party to do so.
L807: 34.15 For the avoidance of doubt, nothing in this Article requires a provision to be to be construed so as to give effect to the commercial intention of the Parties where the requirement would be inconsistent with any mandatory legal or regulatory obligation binding on the Party concerned; in that event the Party shall notify the other and the Parties shall agree a compliant alternative.

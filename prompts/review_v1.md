# Contract review instruction v1

Compare candidate JSON with its exact controlling source clauses. Review every accepted rate/unit and predicate, dates/precedence, per-step half-up rounding, context/threshold scope, units and required dependency closure. Distinguish structurally valid from semantically supported. Retain raw-to-accepted differences and specific evidence for every correction.

For genuinely ambiguous rules or corrected amounts, record alternatives and affected scope. Abstain where necessary facts cannot establish a complete opinion. A source-supported invariant result under all relevant interpretations can be retained with that reasoning. Do not use expected label amounts as a rule oracle. This is author source review, not an independent reviewer or legal certification.
